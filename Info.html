🎲Basic Rules of WhiteJacklynn[BlackJack]

📍Objective:

°WhiteJacklynn—A game of 21's.
°Beat the dealer by having a hand value closer to 21 without exceeding it.

📍Card values:

° Number cards= thier face value
° Face cards(J, Q, K)= 10
° Ace= 1 0r 11 (whichever benefits the hand)

📍Setup:

°Each player places a bet, then receives two cards face up.
°Cards: Displays the cards drawn
°Sum: Displays the total sum of players cards

📍Player Options:

° New Card: Take another card.
° Face cards(J, Q, K)= 10
° Ace= 1 0r 11 (whichever benefits the hand)
